# Employee Expense and Travel Policy (excerpt)

## 1. Reimbursement window

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims
submitted after 30 days require written approval from a department head, and
claims older than 90 days will not be processed under any circumstances.

Receipts are required for any single item above ₹500. Below that threshold a
line entry in the expense tool is sufficient.

## 2. Travel

Domestic economy airfare is reimbursed at actual cost. Upgrades are reimbursed
only for flights with a scheduled duration above six hours, and only with prior
approval. Hotel spend is capped at ₹6,000 per night in metro cities and ₹4,000
elsewhere.

Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at ₹12 per kilometre. Fuel receipts are not
required where the per-kilometre rate is claimed, since the rate is inclusive.

## 3. Client entertainment

Client meals are reimbursable for full-time employees up to ₹3,000 per head with
the client's name and company recorded on the claim. Contractors and agency
staff are not eligible to claim client entertainment, though they may claim
their own meals while travelling under the standard per diem.

Alcohol is not reimbursable in any category.

## 4. Per diem

The standard domestic per diem is ₹1,800 per day for metro locations and ₹1,200
elsewhere, covering meals and incidentals. Per diem is not payable on days where
meals are provided by the client or included in conference registration.
