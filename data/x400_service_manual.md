# X400 Industrial Pump — Service Manual (excerpt)

## 1. Warranty

The X400 pump assembly carries a warranty of 24 months from the date of
commissioning, or 3,000 operating hours, whichever comes first. The warranty
covers manufacturing defects in the impeller housing, drive shaft and seal
assembly. It does not cover wear items such as gaskets, O-rings or filter
cartridges. Warranty is void if the unit is operated outside the rated
temperature envelope of -10°C to 85°C.

The older X300 series carried a 12 month warranty. Units commissioned before
March 2024 remain on the X300 terms even where an X400 impeller was retrofitted.

## 2. Scheduled maintenance

Before the first 500 hours of operation, the inlet filter must be replaced and
the seal torque re-checked to 42 Nm. Skipping the 500 hour filter change is the
single most common cause of premature seal failure in field returns.

Subsequent filter changes are due every 1,500 hours. Lubricant should be
replaced annually regardless of hours.

## 3. Fault codes

- E-114 — inlet pressure below threshold. Check for a blocked strainer.
- E-217 — coolant temperature sensor open circuit or failure. The unit derates
  to 60% flow and will not restart until the sensor is replaced.
- E-302 — drive overcurrent. Usually indicates impeller fouling.
- E-411 — firmware checksum mismatch. Reflash from the service laptop.

Note that E-218, not E-217, indicates a coolant *level* fault. These two are
frequently confused in field reports.

## 4. Storage

Units held in storage for more than six months should have the shaft rotated by
hand a quarter turn every 30 days to prevent seal set.
